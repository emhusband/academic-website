Meaning as a cognitive representation

A slowly changing network in which representations emerge, connect, drift and fade. The three regions remain distinct without implying a rigid sequence or hierarchy.

The animation is intentionally restrained: nodes drift slowly, change size and opacity, form and lose connections, and are replaced over time. A reduced-motion setting produces a quiet static state instead.
